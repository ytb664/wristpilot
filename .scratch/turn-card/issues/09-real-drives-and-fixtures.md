## Found on the drives

- **2026-10-11, first drives on `main`:** distance and street were right but no turn showed: the watch drew no arrow (CANVAS `drawRect` takes corners, not x/y/w/h) and, thinking one was shown, hid the instruction. Fixed in `81a1617`; the user then confirmed the arrows show and "the direction works perfectly", and later "the arrows is good" (ticked in ticket 04 for sharpness, delay and night; sunlight is still open, as no drive since the fix was in daylight).
- **Replaying the six recordings (2026-10-11):** the Turn Card matched Google Maps' distance, street and arrow at every snapshot. Two buzz problems, each now a test case and fixed: (1) ticket 05's open question: the "0 m" → "100 m" jump with the same arrow and street is a **second turn** (the trip's remaining distance keeps falling while it counts down again), seen twice; it got no buzz at all, now it buzzes as a new Maneuver (a jump of 50 m or more after "0 m"; creeping on to "10 m", "20 m" past a turn is not one). (2) After reroutes, "Rerouting...", the "0 m" placeholder and the real Maneuver each buzzed, under a second apart, which feels like the Turn-Now Alert; now "Rerouting..." isn't a Maneuver, and the placeholder's buzz waits 2 s and is dropped if the real Maneuver comes first (a first try, dropping any new-Maneuver buzz within 2 s of another, silenced the real Maneuver; the code review caught it). The replay tests read their expectations straight off each recording, and fail on the core as it was before this ticket.
